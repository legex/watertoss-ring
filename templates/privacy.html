Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025  ·  App: Waterful Ring Toss  ·  Package: com.watertoss.app

1. Information We Collect

Waterful Ring Toss collects only the information you voluntarily provide:

  • Player name — entered when you save a score to the leaderboard. This name is stored on our server and displayed publicly on the leaderboard.
  • Score data — your high score, level reached, and rings scored, saved when you submit a score.

We do not collect names, email addresses, device identifiers, location data, or any other personal information beyond what is listed above.

2. Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob to show interstitial and rewarded ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers and other data as described in Google's Privacy Policy. You can opt out of personalised ads through your device's ad settings.

3. Local Storage

The app stores game progress (completed levels, lives, scores) in your device's local storage. This data never leaves your device unless you choose to submit a score to the leaderboard.

4. Leaderboard

Scores submitted to the global leaderboard are stored on a server hosted on Railway. The player name and score you submit are publicly visible. Do not enter real personal information as your player name.

5. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Data Deletion

To remove your leaderboard entry, contact us at the email below and include your player name. Local game data can be cleared at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing app data in your device settings.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact

Questions? Reach us at: support@watertoss.app
